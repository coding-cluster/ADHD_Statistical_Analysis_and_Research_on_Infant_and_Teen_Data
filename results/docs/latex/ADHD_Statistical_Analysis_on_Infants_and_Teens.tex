\section{Introduction}
% Context, prior literature, gap, research questions / hypotheses
Attention Deficit and Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) is classified as a neurodevelopment disorder that affects attention-span, difficults controlling impulsive behaviour, and complicates remaining in a state of little-to-no activity~\cite{nlm_adhd}.

This is one of the most common disorders affecting children, and it can lead to various problems such as low self-esteem and difficulties with their abilities when not properly treated. Failing to address it can have repercussions throughout life, where in adulthood, people with ADHD may experience low self-esteem, sensitivity to criticism, and increased self-criticism~\cite{apa_adhd}.

In the present work, an analysis design with a sampling plan will be defined to carry out a statistical analysis on datasets (without labeling or names of the participants) of children and adolescents with and without this disorder, in order to subsequently carry out a data statistical analysis process that allows us to understand this neurobiological condition.
